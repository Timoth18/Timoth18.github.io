Privacy policy: Content Office (Coin Town)

Content Office is a private tool run by the owner of the Coin Town channels (TikTok @cointown.sim, YouTube @cointownsim) to upload their own videos.

  • It signs in only to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts, with the owner's permission.
  • It uses that access only to upload videos the owner made, and to read basic account info and post status.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • It does not collect data from viewers or other users.
  • To revoke access, remove "Content Office" at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in TikTok's settings under Security > Manage app permissions.
