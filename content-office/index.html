Content Office

The production and publishing tool behind Coin Town.

Content Office is the software Coin Town uses to turn a finished script into a finished short video and publish it to Coin Town's own channels. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and is used only by the Coin Town team.

What it does

  • Plans each video from a current, sourced news item.
  • Produces the video: narration, subtitles, original animation and music, rendered as a vertical 1080×1920 MP4.
  • Checks facts, length, audio and subtitles before release.
  • Publishes the finished video to TikTok (@cointown.sim) and YouTube (@cointownsim) with a caption, hashtags and the AI-generated content label.

How it uses TikTok

Content Office uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to upload videos to the Coin Town TikTok account only, after the account owner signs in and grants permission. It reads basic account info (display name and avatar) to confirm the right account is connected, and reads the status of each upload. It does not read, store or post anything for any other TikTok user.

Who can use it

Content Office is not a public service and has no public sign-up. Its only user is the owner of the Coin Town channels.
