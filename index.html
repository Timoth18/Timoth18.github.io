How a video is made

We run a small production pipeline we call App. Every step is checked before anything is published.

  1. ResearchPick a current news item and collect the numbers from public, citable sources.
  2. ScriptWrite a short, clear script with a hook, the explanation and the takeaway.
  3. ProduceAnimate original visuals, add narration, subtitles and original music.
  4. Review & postCheck facts and quality, label AI-generated content, then upload to our own channels.

Questions

The short answers.

Is this financial advice?

No. Coin Town is education and entertainment. We explain how things work and show the numbers; decisions about your own money are yours.

Where do the numbers come from?

From public sources such as official statistics, central bank data, company filings and reputable news. Each video's caption names its sources.

Do you use AI?

Yes. Scripts, voice and visuals are produced with AI tools inside our Content Office pipeline, and every post is labelled as AI-generated on TikTok and YouTube.

What is Content Office?

Our own production and upload tool. It posts finished Coin Town videos to our own TikTok and YouTube accounts. Read more.
