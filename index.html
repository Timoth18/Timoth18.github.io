What we make

Short, printed-paper money stories

A real question

Every video starts from something in the news: the rupiah, gold prices, palm oil, the state budget. Then it asks "what if this happened to you?"

Real numbers

Exchange rates, inflation and prices come from public records and major news. Each video lists its sources in the caption.

Education only

We explain how money works. We never tell anyone what to buy, and every video says it is not financial advice.

How it works

Content Office, our publishing tool

Content Office is the small tool the Coin Town team uses to publish its own videos. It is not a service for other creators, and it does not access anyone else's account.

  1. We make the video. The script, voice and animation are produced on our own computer, and checked before posting.
  2. We sign in with TikTok. The channel owner connects the @cointown.sim account once, through TikTok's official Login Kit, and can remove access at any time.
  3. The video is uploaded. Content Office sends the finished video to our own TikTok account using TikTok's Content Posting API, with its caption and an AI-generated content label.
  4. We check the result. It reads the post status so we know the upload finished. It does not read viewers' data.
