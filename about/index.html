About Coin Town

Coin Town is a faceless education channel in Bahasa Indonesia about money. Each short video takes one surprising "what if" (going back to 1998 with today's salary, a rupiah at a new low, a gold price jump) and walks through what would really happen, step by step, with real numbers.

Why

Money news is full of big numbers that are hard to feel. We turn them into everyday pictures: a salary, a price tag, a receipt. The goal is that anyone can understand inflation, exchange rates and debt in under a minute and a half.

Our rules

  • Every number comes from a public source (BPS, Bank Indonesia, official records, major media) and the sources go in the caption.
  • Public figures are discussed only with public-record facts, stated plainly, never as accusations.
  • No investment picks and no financial advice.
  • Videos are made with AI tools and are labelled as AI-generated on every platform.
